# Triangulation Mapping: Claim Validation Visual Logic
**How to Verify Claims Through Independent Sources**

---

## What Is Triangulation Mapping?

Triangulation mapping shows **which sources support which claims** and
**whether those sources are truly independent**. It reveals:
- ✓ Strong triangulation (multiple independent sources, different
  personas)
- ⚠ Weak triangulation (multiple sources, same persona/bias)
- ❌ No triangulation (single-sourced claims)

---

**Analysis:**
- Claims 1 & 4: strong triangulation (3–4 sources, multiple personas)

## Independence Checklist

For each claim, verify source independence by checking:

| Test | Question | Red Flag |
|------|----------|----------|
| **Persona diversity** | Do sources represent different viewpoints? | All same persona type |
| **Bias diversity** | Do sources have different potential biases? | All same incentive structure |
| **Evidence origin** | Is evidence gathered independently? | All citing same original source |
| **Attribution chain** | Are sources original or derivative? | Multiple sources = 1 underlying source |
| **Publication independence** | Are sources from separate entities? | All from same parent organization |

**Example of FALSE independence:**
- Source A: a press release
- Source B: a news article citing that press release
- Source C: a blog post citing the news article

→ This looks like 3 sources but is actually **1 source cited 3 times**.

## Triangulation Scoring Guide

| Score | Sources per Claim | Persona Diversity | Independence | Pattern |
|-------|------------------|-------------------|--------------|---------|
| **5/5** | 3+ sources | 3+ different personas | All independent | ✓✓✓ Different personas, different evidence |
| **4/5** | 2-3 sources | 2-3 personas | Mostly independent | ✓✓ Good diversity, minor echo |
| **3/5** | 2 sources | 2 personas | Some overlap | ✓⚠ Adequate but limited |
| **2/5** | 1-2 sources | Same persona | Significant echo | ⚠⚠ Weak verification |
| **1/5** | 1 source | Single persona | No verification | ❌ No triangulation |

## Practical Workflow

**Step 1:** List all key claims (the assertions your report depends on)

**Step 2:** For each claim, list supporting sources with persona and
band

**Step 3:** Verify independence (check persona type, evidence origin,
attribution chain)

**Step 4:** Score triangulation quality (1–5) using the guide above

**Step 5:** Identify gaps and either:
- Add sources to weak claims
- Downgrade claim confidence
- Remove unsupported claims

**Step 6:** Document triangulation in the report (show your work)

## Example: Full Triangulation Map

**Research Question:** Is a regional accounting firm increasing
compliance headcount?

**CLAIM:** "The firm is actively hiring compliance professionals"

| Source | Persona | Band | Evidence | Independence |
|--------|---------|------|----------|--------------|
| Firm's job board | Primary Artifact | Gold | 12 open compliance positions | Direct (what the firm does) |
| Professional-network data | Independent Observer | Silver | 8 compliance hires in 6 months | External verification |
| Recruiter interview | Anonymous Insider | Silver | "We're overwhelmed with compliance searches" | Insider perspective |
| Industry survey | Captured Expert | Gold | "Top accounting firms up 40% compliance staff" | Benchmark context |

**Triangulation Quality:** ✓ **EXCELLENT** (4 sources, 4 personas,
independent evidence chains)

**Confidence Level:** High (claim is robustly supported)

---

**CLAIM:** "This hiring is driven by new e-invoicing regulations"

| Source | Persona | Band | Evidence | Independence |
|--------|---------|------|----------|--------------|
| Firm's press release | Primary Artifact | Gold | Cites e-invoicing as key focus | Direct statement |
| Industry journalism | Independent Observer | Silver | "E-invoicing driving compliance surge" | Independent reporting |

**Triangulation Quality:** ⚠ **ADEQUATE** (2 sources, 2 personas, but
limited depth)

**Confidence Level:** Moderate (likely true but not exhaustively
verified)

---

**CLAIM:** "The firm is industry leader in compliance preparedness"

| Source | Persona | Band | Evidence | Independence |
|--------|---------|------|----------|--------------|
| Firm's website | Primary Artifact | Silver | Claims leadership position | Self-promotion only |

**Triangulation Quality:** ❌ **NONE** (single-sourced, self-serving
claim)

**Confidence Level:** Low (unsupported; should be removed or heavily
qualified)

## Common Triangulation Mistakes

| Mistake | Example | Correction |
|---------|---------|------------|
| **Echo chamber** | 5 news articles all citing the same press release | Count as 1 source, find independent evidence |
| **Persona clustering** | 10 sources, all Well-Meaning Generalists | Diversify persona types |
| **Quantity over quality** | "We have 50 sources!" (all Bronze) | Prioritize Gold/Silver sources for key claims |
| **Assumed independence** | Two authors from the same firm | Verify actual independence of evidence |
| **Missing counter-evidence** | Only sought confirming sources | Actively search for contrary evidence |

---

**Field use:** For every key claim, map sources by persona and verify
independence. Score 1–5. Flag gaps. Document in the report.
