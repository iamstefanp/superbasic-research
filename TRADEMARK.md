# Trademark

**SuperBasic™** is a trademark of Stefan Petcov / Runway Services.

The distinction here is the one Scrum has used for two decades: the method is
open, the name is not.

## What you can do

Everything the licence allows, freely:

- Use these agents, for anything, including commercially
- Modify them, extend them, strip them down
- Redistribute them, including your modified versions (under CC BY-SA 4.0)
- Describe your work accurately: *"based on SuperBasic™ Agents"* or
  *"adapted from the SuperBasic™ methodology"*

No permission needed. That is the point of publishing them.

## What needs permission

Using the **name** in a way that implies this is official, endorsed, or
certified:

- Naming a product "SuperBasic [something]"
- Claiming to be "SuperBasic certified", "SuperBasic official", or
  "SuperBasic compatible"
- Using SuperBasic™ branding on a commercial offering
- Implying Runway Services endorses your work

## Why

If anyone can ship anything under the name, the name stops meaning anything —
and the thing it currently means is *this method, done properly*. Keeping the
name attached to a standard is what keeps it worth adopting.

The ideas are yours. Take them. The name stays fenced so it keeps its
meaning.
